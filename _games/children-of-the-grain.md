- **System**: Cairn 2e (free SRD; no books needed)
- **Players**: 3–5 (written for 4)
- **Length**: 5 hours; book 5½ to be safe
- **Experience**: Beginners welcome; one page of rules
- **Format**: VTT (Foundry) or in-person
- **Characters**: Pregens provided, chosen at the table
- **Style**: Roleplay-heavy, low dice, player-driven; the GM answers only what you ask

> The guild board in Millbrook is empty. A lord’s war over the hills took every sword in town last week, bread has gone up twice this season, and your room at the Drowned Ox is paid through tomorrow and no further.
> The only work left is a notice nobody wanted: a carpenter and his ten-year-old daughter need walking home to Eden’s Rest, half a day east. Five silver now, ten on arrival, a bed and meals. There’s talk of hungry men on the ridge road.

Eden’s Rest is a valley of gold fields in a country where nothing else grows. Its people are kind, its tables are full, and everyone wears a plait of wheat at the wrist. They’d like you to stay for the harvest festival. Both nights.

*Children of the Grain* is a slow-burn folk-horror one-shot about hospitality and hunger. Every table ends differently.

**What to Expect**
- Low combat, high dread.
- Characters are pregenerated with a personal omen each; no prep needed.
- Cairn 2e: rules-light, deadly, no rolls to persuade or perceive. If you can see it, you see it. If you say it, it’s said.
- Safety tools in use. Lines and veils set at the table.

**Content Warnings**

This is a horror game with mature themes. The full list is hidden below because it hints at the story.

**Players Should Bring**

A willingness to be fed, to like people they don’t trust, and to decide things as a group without a lot of time.
